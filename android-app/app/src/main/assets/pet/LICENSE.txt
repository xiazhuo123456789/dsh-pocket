Animation frames in this directory
=================================

These files are derived from the animation assets of the dsh-pet project:

How the files here were produced
--------------------------------

The upstream transparent VP9 WebM clips were decoded at their native 24 fps and
re-encoded as transparent WebP frames by the dsh-dafeiyu project
(https://github.com/QCYTSN/dsh-dafeiyu, MIT), pinned to upstream commit
549fff2ea33c2acfb16612c76912c6137da4a758. Those frames were then resized to 0.7x
(288 x 241), re-encoded as WebP with quality 70, and packed as one `<clip>.bin`
per clip (frames concatenated in order) with the per-frame byte lengths recorded
in `pet-manifest.json`.

The character artwork is AI-generated by the upstream project. Resizing,
re-encoding and repacking changes the file format, not the rights in the artwork.
No warranty is given. The "dsh-pet" name and its project identity belong to their
respective owners.
